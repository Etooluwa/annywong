About

Meet Anny Wang

Worldwide Vocal Coach and
Founder of the Vocal Transformation Studios

Anny Wang is a private vocal coach and founder of Vocal Transformation Studios, helping singers worldwide unlock their voices through a unique approach that combines vocal technique, confidence, and authentic expression.

Known for her online coaching videos showcasing powerful vocal breakthroughs, Anny’s method has gained attention for creating clear, noticeable transformations by helping singers understand their voices and overcome the obstacles holding them back.

Her journey began with years of musical training, including jazz, before expanding into pop, rock, blues, R&B, and gospel. Through exploring different styles and overcoming her own vocal challenges, Anny developed an approach built on one belief: singing should feel natural, powerful, and expressive.

Today, Anny coaches singers of all levels - from beginners discovering their voice to artists preparing for the stage. Her students have achieved major milestones, including signing with record labels, competing on The Voice, and performing on professional stages such as NBA events.

Through private coaching, online programs, and her team of vocal coaches trained in her method, Anny helps singers build healthy technique, confidence, and the freedom to express their voices fully.
